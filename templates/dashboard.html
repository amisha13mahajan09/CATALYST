Category Weightage Evaluation Metric
Academics10.0%Higher preference to engineering SGPA averages over 10th & 12th %; penalty for backlogs
Coding Languages18.0%Number of languages known & depth (Beginner to Expert)
Technical Skills18.0%Knowledge depth across 15 core technical engineering domains
Coding Platforms5.0%Total questions solved across LeetCode, HackerRank, GeeksforGeeks, etc.
LinkedIn Profile0.5%Academic usage & number of technical posts
Research Papers0.5%Number of published research papers
Internships1.0%Previous industrial internship experience
Hackathons2.0%Competitive hackathon participations & achievements
Courses & Certificates4.0%Total verified course completion certificates
Aptitude Skills10.0%Quant, Logical, Verbal & Data Interpretation depth
Communication Skills10.0%GD, Conversation, Interview Speaking & Grammar depth
Foreign Languages1.0%Fluency in non-Indian international languages (Japanese, German, French, etc.)
Projects20.0%Number of GitHub projects & diversity of programming languages used
