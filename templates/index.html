About CATALYST

CATALYST is a comprehensive Placement Readiness Analysis System designed to bridge the gap between engineering curriculum and industry expectations. Created by Amisha Mahajan, Aryan Bhosale, Maithily Tembhurne and Pranav Borse of MMCOE, AIDS Department, CATALYST analyzes student academic records, coding proficiency, technical skills, aptitude abilities, and extra-curricular achievements and provides students and placement coordinators with actionable insights to maximize placement outcomes.
